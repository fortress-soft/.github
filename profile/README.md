# Fortress Software Solutions

Software solutions for businesses and their customers, from Athens.

## Clients

- [BalanceLab](https://balancelab.gr) is a nutrition and wellness practice in Kifissia. We built and maintain the platform its dietitians and clients use: the staff app, client portal, website and tablet app.
- [Elena's Houses](https://elenas-houses.com) rents short-stay apartments in Athens. We built their website.
- [SCS Security](https://scs-security.gr) (Sound Control Security) installs alarms and CCTV in Athens. We built their website.
- Battle Arts Academy is a kick-boxing academy in Kifissia. Their website is coming soon.

## Products

- [Borealis Connect](https://github.com/fortress-soft/borealis-connect) is an open-source, multi-user server edition of the Borealis D&D 5e character builder by TeamBorealis, for groups who play together.
- ProveYourLag is in development. It records network lag while you play and produces a report you can send to your internet provider.
